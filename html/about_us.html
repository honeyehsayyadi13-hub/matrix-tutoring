ABOUT US

Matrix Tutoring is a tutoring centre that firmly believes in helping students succeed at a pace comfortable to them and with curriculum content altered to fit their needs. Each student has personalized session plans that benefits their learning to achieve academic success and strengthen their skills.

With years of experience and specialization, our tutors cover a wide range of subjects. As a result, students receive focused support when tackling challenging subjects, as instructors are matched to their needs. With a strong understanding of a variety of Ontario curriculum courses due to higher education, our tutors are able to provide instructional support to elementary and secondary school students. We assist students with a variety of subjects, including STEM fields and language arts, helping them develop their confidence and improve their academic performance.

Our sessions are customized to each student's needs as we believe that every child is unique, therefore their learning plans should be as well. In addition to this, we provide 1-on-1 sessions if needed as some students benefit from having full attention. Our general sessions are capped at a maximum of three students per tutor to easily monitor our students' learning.

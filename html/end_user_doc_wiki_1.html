General Navigation / How to Get Started

A guide for navigating the website and accessing the available features.

  1. Open the Matrix Tutoring homepage, or click here.

  2. Use the navigation bar to have access to the following: home page, services, contact information, website map, about us, frequently asked questions, login and registration, job application, switch themes, and company login.

  3. To familiarize yourself with our services, head to the navigation bar and select services, a drop down of all the services offered will appear.

Account Creation / Logging In

A guide for creating an account and logging into an account.

  1. Open the Matrix Tutoring homepage, or click here.

  2. Use the navigation bar to find the following: "Sign Up/Log In", or click here.

  3. Fill in the following: Username, Email, and Password.

  4. Click the "Register" button. A confirmation message will pop up on the screen.

  5. To Log In: Put in the same information as the account creation then click the "Log In" button. You will then be sent to the profile page where you can fill out a consultation form or log out.

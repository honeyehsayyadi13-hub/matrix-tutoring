Offerings

Here is a sample of what we offer, please refer to our "About Us" section for our complete list of offerings including the style of tutoring we have available.


ENGLISH

Students are taught both reading and writing in English. All grade levels are permitted to bring in their own work to have reviewed and improved upon by tutors. Targeted towards young children who need their reading and writing skills improved upon.

SCIENCE

Tutors who specialize in fields such as biology, chemistry, and physics support students in these areas. Students in high school can get the necessary help such as exam and test preparation to pass courses with successful results.

FRENCH

Students who need assistance in French can be guided by French tutors who support all subjects in the language. Altered to each grade level.



HOMEWORK

Geared towards students who need support with their homework, assignments, or test preparations. Students are encouraged to bring class material to complete their work while tutors help them create above satisfactory work.

MATH

Aimed towards students of all grade levels. Those enrolled will improve upon their skills and gain the confidence needed to perform well on their assessments. High school students can be taught by tutors who specialize in mathematics.

CODING

Students who are looking for assistance in learning a new skill are paired with expert tutors and guided through sessions that enable students to learn the basics of coding languages.

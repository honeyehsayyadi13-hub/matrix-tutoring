Filling Out a Job Application Form

A guide for filling in the job application form.

  1. Open the Matrix Tutoring homepage, or click here.

  2. Use the navigation bar to find the following: "Apply Now", or click here.

  3. Fill in the following to submit an application: name, phone number, email (must be in an acceptible format: doc, pdf, docx), resume, and a message that describes what you are able to teach.

  4. When done filling out the form, select the "Submit Request" button.

  5. When the submission is successfully sent, the user is sent to a "Thank You" page where they can click buttons that either let them submit another form or redirects them to the home page.

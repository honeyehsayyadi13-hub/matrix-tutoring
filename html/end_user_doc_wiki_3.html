Creating Consultation Requests

A guide for filling in the form to create a request for a consultation.

  1. Open the Matrix Tutoring homepage, or click here.

  2. Use the navigation bar to find the following: "Sign Up/Log In", or click here.

  3. Fill in the following to log into your account: Username, Email, and Password.

  4. Log into your account by selecting the "Log In" button. If you have not created account, click here for the guide.

  5. Once logged in, select the "Book Consultation" button.

  6. Fill in the following information: student's name, parent's name, phone number, subject, style, email, and a message about what the company should know about the child before the consultation.

  7. For the subject and style, a drop down menu is shown of the options, pick the one that suits your child's needs the most.

  8. When done, select the "Submit Request" button. You will be sent back to the profile page.

Frequently Asked Questions

If you have any questions, please refer to our email to ask.



How can I book a consultation and what is the proccess?

You can click here to book a consultation. Once submitted, a response via email will be sent about scheduling a free assessment.

Are online sessions available?

We are able to offer online synchronous tutoring for students who prefer learning online. More information can be found if asked about this through our email.

What grade levels and subjects do you offer to support?

Matrix tutoring is able to support students from grade 1 to 12 in almost all subject areas and help with homework, projects, assignments, and exam prep for students in secondary school.

What are the tutoring rates?

The rates are discussed either in person or on email. It is determined by your child's learning plan.
